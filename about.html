About North Star Bakery

Our Neighborhood Story

North Star Bakery was founded in 2018 with a simple purpose: to restore honest, traditional artisan baking to our local neighborhood. What began as a modest stall at the weekend farmers market has grown into a brick-and-mortar hub where neighbors meet, families share morning treats, and commuters pick up fresh bread for dinner.

Sourcing Philosophy

Great bread begins with clean, unhurried ingredients. We partner directly with regional organic grain mills that grind heritage wheat slowly to preserve natural nutrients. Our dairy comes from family-owned pasture-raised cows, and our sourdough starter has been meticulously maintained daily for over eight years without chemical enhancers or industrial yeast.

Our Baking Team

  • Elena Vance – Head Baker & Co-Founder: Specializes in long-cold-fermentation sourdough breads and heritage grains.
  • Marcus Chen – Pastry Chef: Classically trained in French viennoiserie, crafting delicate lamination and seasonal fruit tarts.
  • Sarah Lindqvist – Community & Front of House: Manages daily counter service, customer catering pre-orders, and local neighborhood partnerships.
